Privacy Policy

Your data stays on device

All user data is stored locally on your device. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information.

01

Introduction

Welcome to LexiNovaOne. We take your privacy seriously. LexiNovaOne is a local educational utility for semiconductor study, including P-type and N-type carrier estimates, PN junction analysis, diode bias and capacitance calculations, diffusion length, MOSFET threshold and Id–Vgs exploration, band diagrams, study quizzes, and related reference material. Before using the app, please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By tapping Continue, you confirm that you have read this policy.

LexiNovaOne provides on-device calculation and study tools only.

02

Information We Collect

LexiNovaOne works entirely on your device. We do not collect personal information from you over the internet. The app may store the following information locally on your device:

  • Calculation inputs you enter in the Lab workbench, such as temperature, doping levels, material parameters, bias voltages, geometry values, and other numeric fields for the selected mode;
  • Calculation results produced on your device, including carrier concentrations, junction properties, MOSFET estimates, formula breakdowns, and related summaries;
  • Saved run history records, timestamps, mode identifiers, input snapshots, and result text when you perform calculations;
  • App preferences stored locally, such as default material preset (Si, Ge, GaAs), lab defaults, decimal precision, typical-range hint visibility, and feature tour completion state;
  • Study progress and quiz selections while you use Study tools on the device;
  • Content you explicitly export or share through the iOS share sheet when you choose to copy or send calculation results.
03

How We Use Information

We use information stored on your device solely to provide and improve LexiNovaOne features, including:

  • Running semiconductor calculations across supported Lab modes;
  • Displaying formula references, band diagrams, Id–Vgs plots, and study quiz content;
  • Maintaining your History list, search, compare, and record detail views;
  • Presenting glossary terms and reference material in Settings;
  • Applying your saved lab defaults, material presets, and display preferences;
  • Copying or sharing results when you explicitly choose to do so.

We do not sell your personal information or use it for purposes unrelated to the app without your consent.

04

Storage and Protection

Your data is stored locally on your device using iOS storage mechanisms, including the app sandbox, NSUserDefaults for preferences, and local files in the app's Application Support directory. Calculation history, parameters, and preferences remain on your device until you delete individual records in History, use Clear Inputs in the Lab, use Clear All History in Settings, export and remove copies yourself, or uninstall LexiNovaOne.

We take reasonable measures to help protect information on your device. However, no electronic storage method is completely secure. Please protect your device with a passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID.

05

Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We do not transmit your calculation inputs, results, or history to our servers. We may share information only when:

  • You have given explicit consent, such as when you copy a result or use the iOS share sheet to save or send content to a destination you choose;
  • Disclosure is required by applicable law or a competent authority;
  • Disclosure is necessary to protect the legitimate rights and interests of you, us, or other users.
06

Your Rights

You may:

  • View, search, compare, and delete individual history records in the History tab;
  • Clear all saved calculation runs from Settings → Clear All History;
  • Reset current Lab inputs with Clear Inputs without deleting history;
  • Change lab defaults, material preset, and display options in Settings;
  • Replay the feature tour from Settings when you want a guided overview;
  • Uninstall the app to remove locally stored data from your device.

To ask questions or request assistance, please contact us as described in Section 9.

07

Protection of Minors

LexiNovaOne is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a minor, please read this policy with your guardian and use the app only with their consent. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, please contact us.

08

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the latest version. Continued use of LexiNovaOne after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
